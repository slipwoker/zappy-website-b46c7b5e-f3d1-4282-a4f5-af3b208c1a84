Trust & Safety FAQ.

How does escrow work?

Velora holds payment securely until delivery is confirmed by both buyer and driver. Funds are released only after successful completion, protecting both parties from fraud and ensuring fair transactions across our digital freight marketplace.

What is required for driver verification?

All drivers must submit valid ID, vehicle registration, insurance proof, and pass a background check before accepting deliveries. This multi-step verification ensures only qualified, trustworthy drivers operate on our platform.

What triggers the 3‑strike policy?

Strikes are issued for late deliveries without notice, confirmed item damage, unprofessional conduct, or privacy violations. Three strikes result in permanent account suspension to maintain platform quality and user trust.

Can users share personal contact details?

No. Velora uses a signal private messenger-style system where all communication stays in-app. Sharing phone numbers, emails, or social media handles is prohibited to protect privacy and maintain secure transaction records.

How are disputes handled?

Our support team reviews transaction history, in-app messages, and photo evidence within 48 hours. Escrow funds remain frozen during investigation. Fair resolutions include refunds, partial credits, or driver penalties based on verified facts.

Where does Velora operate?

Velora exclusively serves North Carolina and South Carolina. Our regional focus allows optimized logistics routes, faster delivery times, and localized customer support tailored to the Carolinas marketplace community.
