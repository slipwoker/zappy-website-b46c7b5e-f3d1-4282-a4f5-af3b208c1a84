How Velora Moves a Delivery From Request to Drop‑Off.

Velora's marketplace and logistics work together through three separated domains—so the experience stays fast while permissions stay tight.

Buyer

Browse items for sale on the marketplace, place orders with escrow-protected payments, and track deliveries in real-time.

Your payment is held securely until the seller confirms shipment and the driver completes delivery.

Seller

List products on the digital freight marketplace, manage inventory, and coordinate pickups through the seller dashboard.

Receive payment once the driver confirms successful delivery to the buyer.

Driver

Accept delivery requests, verify identity through our strict verification system, and earn with transparent per-delivery rates.

Communicate securely with buyers and sellers using our signal private messenger-style in-app messaging.

The End‑to‑End Path.

01

Create a request or purchase

Browse items for sale on the digital freight marketplace or post your own request. Velora connects buyers and sellers across North and South Carolina in a secure environment.

02

Escrow holds funds

Payment is securely held in escrow until delivery is complete. This protects both buyers and sellers, ensuring trust throughout the transaction process.

03

Seller confirms readiness

The seller prepares the item and confirms it's ready for pickup. This triggers the next phase, notifying available drivers in the area to accept the delivery request.

04

Driver accepts and verifies pickup

A verified driver accepts the delivery. All drivers undergo background checks and operate under a strict 3-strike policy to maintain service quality and safety standards.

05

Delivery with status updates

Track your delivery in real-time with live status updates. Communicate privately through our secure messaging system powered by signal private messenger protocols.

06

Completion and release of funds

Once delivery is confirmed, funds are automatically released from escrow to the seller. Rate your experience to help maintain the quality of our marketplace community.

Three Domains, One Data Source.

Separation reduces risk and simplifies permissions without slowing down operations.

  • Least-privilege access
  • Clear audit boundaries
  • Faster incident containment

Guest Requests via QR Codes.

Velora can accept requests from outside the app—so customers can start a delivery flow instantly.

Fast entry point

Customers scan a QR code at your location and immediately begin a delivery request without downloading the app.

Clear handoff

The external request seamlessly transitions into Velora's verified driver network with full tracking and escrow protection.

Same trust rules

Guest requests follow the same 3-strike driver policy, escrow payments, and private messaging standards as in-app orders.
